Printed in the quiz paper

Both recent Quiz 1 papers opened with a formula file containing: a discrete table (Uniform, Bernoulli, Binomial, Geometric, Poisson: PMF, CDF, mean, variance), a continuous table (Uniform, Exponential, Normal, Gamma, Beta: PDF, CDF, mean, variance), and the statements of Markov's and Chebyshev's inequalities.

Joint, marginal, conditionalWeek 1

  • Joint PMF\[f_{XY}(t_1,t_2)=P(X=t_1,Y=t_2),\quad \textstyle\sum\sum f_{XY}=1\]
  • Marginal (sum out the other)\[f_X(t_1)=\sum_{t_2}f_{XY}(t_1,t_2)\]
  • Conditional (row ÷ row total)\[f_{Y\mid X=t_1}(t_2)=\frac{f_{XY}(t_1,t_2)}{f_X(t_1)}\]
  • Factorisation / chain rule\[f_{XYZ}=f_{X\mid Y,Z}\;f_{Y\mid Z}\;f_Z\]
  • Poisson splitting: N ~ Poisson(λ), X | N = n ~ Bin(n, p)\[X\sim\text{Poisson}(\lambda p),\ \ N-X\sim\text{Poisson}(\lambda(1-p))\ \text{indep.}\]

IndependenceWeek 2

  • Definition (every cell)\[f_{XY}(t_1,t_2)=f_X(t_1)f_Y(t_2)\ \ \forall t_1,t_2\]
  • Equivalent\[f_{Y\mid X=t_1}(t_2)=f_Y(t_2)\]
  • i.i.d. samples, all exceed c\[P(X_1\gt c,\dots,X_n\gt c)=P(X\gt c)^n\]
  • Geometric memoryless\[P(X\gt m+n\mid X\gt m)=(1-p)^n\]
  • Quick dependence testA zero cell whose row and column totals are both non-zero.

Sums of random variablesWeek 2

  • Convolution\[P(X+Y=z)=\sum_x f_{XY}(x,z-x)\]
  • Independent Poissons\[X+Y\sim\text{Poi}(\lambda_1+\lambda_2),\ \ X\mid X{+}Y{=}n\sim\text{Bin}\!\left(n,\tfrac{\lambda_1}{\lambda_1+\lambda_2}\right)\]
  • Binomials with the same p\[X_1\mid X_1{+}X_2{=}m\sim\text{HyperGeo}(n_1{+}n_2,\,n_1,\,m)\]
  • i.i.d. Geometric(p)\[P(X+Y=n)=(n-1)p^2(1-p)^{n-2},\ \ X\mid X{+}Y{=}n\sim\text{Unif}\{1..n{-}1\}\]
  • Two fair dice\[P(X+Y=s)=\tfrac{6-|s-7|}{36},\quad P(X-Y=d)=\tfrac{6-|d|}{36}\]

Max and minWeek 2

  • Max of independent\[P(\max\le k)=\prod_i P(X_i\le k)\]
  • Min of independent\[P(\min\ge k)=\prod_i P(X_i\ge k)\]
  • i.i.d., integer values\[P(\max=k)=F(k)^n-F(k-1)^n\]
  • Min of independent Geometrics\[\min\sim\text{Geo}\big(1-(1-p_1)(1-p_2)\big)\]
  • Two fair dice\[P(\max=k)=\tfrac{2k-1}{36},\quad P(\min=k)=\tfrac{13-2k}{36}\]

Expectation and varianceWeek 3

  • Mean of a function\[E[g(X,Y)]=\sum\sum g(t_1,t_2)f_{XY}(t_1,t_2)\]
  • Linearity (always)\[E[aX+bY+c]=aE[X]+bE[Y]+c\]
  • Variance\[\mathrm{Var}(X)=E[X^2]-(E[X])^2,\ \ \mathrm{Var}(aX+b)=a^2\mathrm{Var}(X)\]
  • Sum\[\mathrm{Var}(X\pm Y)=\mathrm{Var}X+\mathrm{Var}Y\pm2\mathrm{Cov}(X,Y)\]
  • Independent product\[\mathrm{Var}(XY)=E[X^2]E[Y^2]-(E[X]E[Y])^2\]
  • Hypergeometric (not printed)\[E=\tfrac{mr}{N},\ \ \mathrm{Var}=m\tfrac rN\tfrac{N-r}{N}\tfrac{N-m}{N-1}\]

Covariance and correlationWeek 3

  • Covariance\[\mathrm{Cov}(X,Y)=E[XY]-E[X]E[Y]\]
  • Bilinear\[\mathrm{Cov}(aX+bY,Z)=a\,\mathrm{Cov}(X,Z)+b\,\mathrm{Cov}(Y,Z)\]
  • Correlation\[\rho=\frac{\mathrm{Cov}(X,Y)}{\mathrm{SD}(X)\mathrm{SD}(Y)}\in[-1,1]\]
  • Scaling\[\rho(aX+b,cY+d)=\mathrm{sign}(ac)\,\rho(X,Y)\]
  • Independent ⇒ Cov = 0 (not the other way round)\[\mathrm{Cov}(X,X)=\mathrm{Var}(X),\ \ \mathrm{Cov}(X,c)=0\]

BoundsWeek 3 · printed in the paper

  • Markov (X ≥ 0)\[P(X\ge c)\le\frac{\mu}{c}\]
  • Chebyshev\[P(|X-\mu|\ge k\sigma)\le\frac1{k^2},\quad P(|X-\mu|\ge c)\le\frac{\sigma^2}{c^2}\]
  • Lower bound form\[P(|X-\mu|\lt c)\ge1-\frac{\sigma^2}{c^2}\]
  • Integer X, strict inequality\[P(X\gt 50)=P(X\ge51)\le\tfrac{\mu}{51}\]

CDF and PDFWeek 4

  • CDF\[F(x)=P(X\le x),\quad P(a\lt X\le b)=F(b)-F(a)\]
  • Point mass = jump\[P(X=a)=F(a)-F(a^-)\]
  • PDF\[F(x)=\int_{-\infty}^{x}f(u)\,du,\quad f=F'\]
  • Valid density\[f\ge0,\quad \int_{-\infty}^{\infty}f(x)\,dx=1\]
  • Exponential tail · memoryless\[P(X\gt x)=e^{-\lambda x},\ \ P(X\gt s{+}t\mid X\gt s)=e^{-\lambda t}\]
  • Standardise a normal\[P(X\le x)=F_Z\!\left(\tfrac{x-\mu}{\sigma}\right),\ \ F_Z(-z)=1-F_Z(z)\]
